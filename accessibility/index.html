Accessibility Statement

Balanced Ketamine wants every patient to be able to learn about ketamine infusion therapy and request care, whatever their ability. Our goal is for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. If anything on the site is hard to use, call (913) 871-9888 and we will help you directly.

What is our accessibility commitment?

We aim to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) guidance and Section 1557 of the Affordable Care Act. We test new pages before publishing and review the site regularly.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we add pages, videos and documents, we check them against the same standard and fix problems we find.

Which accessibility features does this website include?

  • Keyboard navigation – every menu, button, form, accordion and popup works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Skip link – a “Skip to main content” link appears as the first item when you press Tab.
  • Accessibility tools – the purple button at the edge of the screen lets you enlarge or reduce text, switch to grayscale, high or negative contrast, use a light background, underline links or switch to a more readable font. Your choices are remembered on your device.
  • Screen reader support – semantic headings, landmarks, descriptive link text, form labels, ARIA attributes and alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Readable design – text colors chosen for contrast, resizable text up to 200% without loss of content, and layouts that adapt to phones, tablets and zoomed screens.
  • Reduced motion – animations are turned off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Touch-friendly controls – buttons and links sized for easy tapping on mobile devices.

Is the clinic itself accessible?

Our Overland Park clinic is designed to welcome patients with disabilities. Please tell us when you book if you need any of the following and we will arrange it at no cost to you:

  • Help with building access, mobility needs or seating in the infusion suite;
  • A qualified sign language interpreter or other auxiliary aids;
  • Written materials in large print or another accessible format;
  • Language assistance services for patients with limited English proficiency;
  • Accommodation for a service animal.

Does Balanced Ketamine discriminate on any basis?

No. Balanced Ketamine complies with applicable federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability or sex (including sexual orientation and gender identity). We do not exclude people or treat them differently because of these characteristics.

If you believe we have failed to provide these services or discriminated in another way, you can contact us using the details below, or file a civil rights complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at ocrportal.hhs.gov or 1-800-368-1019 (TDD 1-800-537-7697).

How do I report an accessibility problem?

  1. Note the web page address (URL) and what you were trying to do.
  2. Describe the problem and the assistive technology or browser you were using, if any.
  3. Call (913) 871-9888 or write to us at the address below.
