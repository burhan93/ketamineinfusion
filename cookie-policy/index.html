What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts. They help a site work, remember your settings and, with your permission, measure visits or show embedded content.

How can I change my cookie choices?

When you first visit, a banner lets you Accept All, choose Necessary Only, or set Preferences by category. You can change your choice at any time:

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working.

Do cookies collect my health information?

No. We do not use cookies, pixels or analytics to collect information about your health, symptoms, treatments or appointments, and we do not place tracking technologies on patient-only areas. Please do not enter health information in website forms. How we protect medical information is explained in our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Do you honor Global Privacy Control and “Do Not Sell or Share” requests?

Yes. We do not sell personal information. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and marketing cookies stay switched off. Residents of states with consumer privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others) may also contact us to exercise rights to access, delete or correct personal information, or to opt out. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

How do third parties use cookies?

Google, Meta and YouTube process data under their own privacy policies. You can learn more and set preferences with each provider:

  • Google cookie policy and Google Analytics opt-out
  • Meta cookie policy
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
